> Apply this method within the target project's rules and task authorization. Selecting or reading it does not execute commands or grant additional permissions.

Re-pitch the message that did not land. Give the missing context, use short concrete sentences in the user's language, and explain unfamiliar terms before relying on them. Reuse the project's established vocabulary; if it has a GLOSSARY-MAP.md, follow it to the relevant GLOSSARY.md. A glossary is helpful, not a prerequisite. Keep the underlying answer and its uncertainty accurate rather than adding a new workflow.
